struct Resumption<R, A>

A one-shot suspended effect continuation local to the worker that captured it.

Resuming supplies R at the perform site and returns the handled extent's A. The linear type must be consumed exactly once by resume or cancel; it cannot be copied, dropped implicitly, or transferred to another worker.
